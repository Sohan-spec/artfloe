By the end of this page you will have a restyled photo on [artfloe.fun](https://artfloe.fun).

## Prerequisites

- A photo on your device (JPEG, PNG, BMP, or WebP, under 8 MB)
- An email address you can sign in with

## 1. Open the studio

Go to [https://artfloe.fun](https://artfloe.fun). If you are not signed in, the app redirects you to `/login`.

## 2. Create an account

1. Enter email and password (at least 8 characters).
2. Complete the CAPTCHA if the page shows one.
3. Submit. You land in the studio.

Email/password is the path this page uses. The login screen may also offer Google; if that button reports it is not configured, use email.

## 3. Upload a photo

1. Drag a photo onto **Drag & Drop to Upload Content Image**, or click **Choose File**.
2. Wait until a preview appears in the card.

Uploads are checked with NudeNet before they are stored. A rejected photo does not start GPU work.

## 4. Pick a style

Under **Choose a Style**, click a catalog thumbnail (for example **Starry Night**). You can switch the list between **All** and **By mood**.

## 5. Run Transform

1. Click **Transform**.
2. Watch the overlay until it shows the result.

Success: you see a restyled image. If people were detected, **Subject blend** appears so you can mix a little style onto the preserved person (0–15%). **Original** crop stays selected unless you change it.

A generation uses one slot of your monthly quota (Free 5, Pro 50, Max 150). The count is reserved before GPU work starts.

## 6. Save or crop (optional)

- **1:1**, **4:5**, **9:16**, or **16:9** in the result overlay crop the export around the stored subject box.
- **History** and **Favorites** in the profile menu reopen past generations.
